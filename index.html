About

Eclectic is a small, one‑man operation; a publication that explores the chaotic interiors and forgotten frontiers of the human condition through fiction. Our slogan remains:

One Thing Leads to Another. Everything is Connected.

Eclectic prides itself on the neglected and archaic, favoring the labyrinthine, obsessive, grotesque, and avant‑garde in human expression. We find interest in the reading between the lines, picking through the grit of the unknown and the unknowable.

The primary home of Ward Henderson’s (founder and editor‑in‑chief) short fiction and miscellaneous writings.
